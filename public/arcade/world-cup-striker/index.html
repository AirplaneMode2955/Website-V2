Pick your nation. Slingshot your shots past the keeper. Lift the trophy.

How To Play

⚽ Aim: Press and drag back from the ball, like a slingshot. Release to shoot.

💪 Power: Pull farther back for a harder shot — but too much and you'll blast it over the bar.

🌀 Curve: Flick sideways right as you release to bend the ball around the keeper.

⏱️ Speed: You've got a few seconds per kick before the ref blows the whistle.

🏆 Tournament: Win 5 shootout rounds — Group Stage through the Final — to lift the trophy. Keepers get sharper every round.
